Privacy Policy — Cyclone Kai

Effective date: October 17, 2026. Page address: https://kkoder-cyclone.github.io/cyclone-previews/youtube-app/privacy

1. Summary

  • Cyclone Kai ("the App") uploads videos only to the owner's own two YouTube channels: "Kod pod kapyushonom" and "Planyorka v manezhe".
  • The App requests only the Google OAuth scopes needed to upload and manage the owner's own videos (see section 4).
  • The App does not collect data about any third party.
  • The App does not display ads, and there is no third-party advertising.
  • The App does not sell data or share it for advertising.

2. Who we are

  • Owner and operator: Kirill Vladimirovich Suchkov, self-employed individual (professional income tax payer), Russian Federation ("we", "us").
  • Contact for data questions: kcodex@cyclonev2.com
  • App website: https://kkoder-cyclone.github.io/cyclone-previews/youtube-app/

3. What the App does

  • The App uses YouTube API Services.
  • We use the App to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channels. The owner approves every upload.
  • The App sends the following to YouTube:
  • video file;
  • thumbnail;
  • title;
  • description, including timestamps;
  • tags;
  • category;
  • language;
  • privacy status: private, unlisted, or public (private by default);
  • scheduled publish time (publishAt), if set by the owner;
  • altered or synthetic content disclosure (containsSyntheticMedia);
  • made-for-kids declaration (selfDeclaredMadeForKids, true or false);
  • playlist, if selected by the owner.
  • The App does not access comments, subscribers, or analytics.
  • The App does not delete videos.
  • Only the channel owner uses the App. No other users sign in to it.

4. Google OAuth scopes

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — used only to upload the owner's videos, set thumbnail and publish time, and add videos to the owner's existing playlists. No deletion, video edits or comments.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — optional; used only to add the owner's videos to the owner's playlists and to change the scheduled publish time of the owner's videos.
  • The App requests no other scopes.

5. Data the App accesses and stores

  • We store three OAuth credential values: the OAuth client ID, the client secret, and the refresh token. They are stored only in encrypted form.
  • If the two channels belong to different Google accounts, we store two refresh tokens.
  • We do not store access tokens. An access token is kept only in memory for its lifetime (about 1 hour).
  • After an upload, YouTube returns the video ID, privacy status, and publish time. We keep the video ID and status for no longer than 30 days.
  • The App never receives the Google account password. The owner signs in on Google's own page.
  • The App does not access the user's email address, name, contacts, or payment information.
  • The App does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

6. How we use the data

  • OAuth credentials are used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels.
  • The video ID is used only to locate the uploaded video in YouTube Studio.
  • We use the data for no other purpose.

7. How we store and protect the data

  • OAuth credentials are stored on our server in an encrypted file.
  • Encryption: Fernet (AES-128 symmetric encryption with HMAC integrity check).
  • The encryption key is stored in a separate file.
  • Both files are readable only by the server's service account (file permissions 600, folder permissions 700).
  • The upload program never prints the credentials or writes them to logs.
  • Credentials are never stored in plain text, in chat systems, or in code repositories.
  • All data is sent to Google over HTTPS only.

8. Sharing and disclosure

  • Videos and their metadata are sent only to YouTube, as required to perform the upload.
  • We do not share Google user data with any third party.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We may disclose data only if required by applicable law.
  • The App does not serve ads and does not allow third parties to serve ads or content in the App.

9. Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: - We use Google user data only to provide the App's user-facing feature: uploading and managing videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. - We do not transfer Google user data to others, except as necessary to provide this feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to users. - We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving ads, including personalized or interest-based advertising. - We do not sell Google user data to data brokers or information resellers. - We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. - No human reads Google user data, except with the user's affirmative consent, for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), to comply with applicable law, or when the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations. - We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

11. Retention, revocation, and deletion

  • We keep the OAuth credentials until access is revoked.
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • When we delete data, we first revoke the refresh token with Google programmatically.
  • After access is revoked, we delete the encrypted credential file and its encryption key within 7 days.
  • To request deletion of stored data, email kcodex@cyclonev2.com. We delete the data within 7 days of the request.
  • Videos already uploaded remain on the channels; the channel owner can delete them in YouTube Studio.
